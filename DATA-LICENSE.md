This project keeps software licensing separate from data licensing.

Any approved distribution of a derivative database must preserve attribution, identify the ODbL, and satisfy applicable share-alike and database-availability obligations.

Operational SQLite checkpoints are private internal state and are not a public data distribution. They contain identity mappings, source evidence, raw provider payloads, observations, errors, and historical state.

Publicly reachable ATS vacancy pages may have separate provider, employer, copyright, database-right, privacy, and terms-of-use constraints. Public accessibility alone is not treated as permission to redistribute full descriptions or raw payloads. Public job exports remain disabled until a field-level redistribution policy is approved.
